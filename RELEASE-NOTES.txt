First public release (fixes the macOS "damaged" message on Apple Silicon).

- Cursorchains, cursorbits and cursor emitters hang from, orbit and drift off your mouse cursor, with a 3D rig editor.
- Nothing is pre-loaded: import the .crig collection attached to this release (or your own .crig files).
- Double-click any .crig file to open it in Cursor Rig.
- Updates are signed and installed from inside the app (About > Check for updates).
